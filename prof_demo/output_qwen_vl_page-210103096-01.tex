\subsection*{Question 1}

Given,
\[ S = \left\{
-1, -8.5, -2, 0.5, 2.5, 3, 2, 4, 1
\right\}
and A = \left[
\begin{array}{rrrrr}
-1 & 8.5 & -2 & 0.5 \\
4.5 & 2.5 & -3 & 3 \\
-0.5 & -1.5 & 3 & 1
\end{array}
\right]

A) To show S is a subspace of M\_n(R), we need to verify that it satisfies the following conditions:

1. The zero matrix must be in S.
2. If \( x \in S \) and \( y \in S \), then \( x + y \in S \).
3. If \( x \in S \) and \( y \in S \), then \( xy \in S \).

Let's check each condition.

1. \textbf{The zero matrix must be in S:}
   \[ \text{Zero matrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \]
   \[ \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 8.5 \\ -2 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 8.5 \\ -2 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 17 \\ -4 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix} \]
   Since \(\begin{pmatrix} -2 & 17 \\ -4 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix}\) is not in S, the zero matrix is not in S.

2. \textbf{If \( x \in S \) and \( y \in S \), then \( x + y \in S \):}
   Let \( x = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -8.5 \end{pmatrix} \) and \( y = \begin{pmatrix} 0.5 \\ 2.5 \end{pmatrix} \)
   \[ x + y = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -8.5 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 0.5 \\ 2.5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -0.5 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix} \]
   Since \(-0.5\) is not in S, \(x + y \neq S\).

3. \textbf{If \( x \in S \) and \( y \in S \), then \( xy \in S \):}
   Let \( x = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -8.5 \end{pmatrix} \) and \( y = \begin{pmatrix} 0.5 \\ 2.5 \end{pmatrix} \)
   \[ xy = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 8.5 \\ -8.5 & 0.5 \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 0.5 \\ 2.5 \end{pmatrix} \]
   Since \( xy \notin S \), condition 3 is not satisfied.

Therefore, \( S \) is not a subspace of \( M_n(\mathbb{R}) \).
